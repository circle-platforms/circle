Hand the user this conversation's browser and wait until they hand it back.

Usage:
- Use it for what only the user should do in the page: a CAPTCHA, a security key or a code sent to their phone, a password they have not saved as a secret, a choice that is theirs to make.
- `reason` tells them what to do, in a sentence.
- While they have the browser you see nothing of it. When they hand it back, the result lists where the tabs went meanwhile and shows the page as it is now; it never shows what they typed.
- The user can also take the browser on their own; your next browser action then waits for them, does nothing, and shows the page as they left it.

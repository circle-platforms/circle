Hand the user a device terminal and wait until they are done with it.

Usage:
- Use it only for what the user must do themselves on the device: a password they have not saved as a secret, a console menu only a person may walk, an out-of-band code.
- `reason` tells them what to do, in a sentence. The terminal page opens on the device so they can type at once.
- While they hold it your actions wait; nothing is sent. Their turn ends when they give it back or send you a message. The result shows the screen as they left it and says what they did, never what they typed: words come back only as the device echoed them on its own.
- The user can take a terminal on their own too; your next action on it then waits for them and shows where they left it.
- Plan mode does not hand a terminal over.

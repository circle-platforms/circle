Click a visible control that has no ref, using the latest browser_screenshot. Give its screenshotId and x/y within that screenshot's reported CSS viewport dimensions. If the image was scaled, map its image coordinates to the CSS viewport first. A screenshot stays current while the spot you click stays as the picture shows it: a page that redraws another part of itself, as a dashboard with polling charts does, needs no new picture. Take a new one after navigation, switching tabs, resizing, scrolling, or a change at or under the point. Prefer browser_click with a ref whenever available. Circle sends real pointer input and reports the target actually hit. Unknown and destructive targets need the person's approval once in manual mode. Certificate warning pages require their ref controls and cannot be clicked with coordinates. Coordinate clicks are not recorded into browser scripts.
